Team challenge: five lenses, one decision

For a small team working together. One person drives the screen.

How this works

Choose one person to click for the whole team and share their screen. Everyone else follows along.

For each dilemma:

  1. Decide alone (30 seconds). Read the five options and pick yours silently.
  2. Discuss. Take turns saying which option you chose and why.
  3. Agree. Choose the option that matches most of your team's answers, or one everyone can live with.

Most teams finish four dilemmas in 15 minutes. If you are quick, keep going: there are six. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere.
