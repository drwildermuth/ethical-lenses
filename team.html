Team challenge: what to do, and why

For a small team working together. One person drives the screen.

How this works

Choose one person to click for the whole team and share their screen. Everyone else follows along.

Each of the three dilemmas has two steps:

  1. What do we do? Pick the action your team would take.
  2. Why? Pick the reason closest to the reasoning behind your choice. Two teams can pick the same action for different reasons.

For each step:

  • Decide alone first (30 seconds). Choose silently.
  • Discuss. Take turns saying what you chose and why.
  • Agree. Choose the option that matches most of your team, or one everyone can live with.

If nothing fits, there is a last option to write your own. Please look for the closest match first. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere.
